# Ảnh minh hoạ sân bóng

- File dùng trong ứng dụng: `mini-football.png`.
- Tạo bằng công cụ imagegen tích hợp (built-in), không dùng CLI/API key.
- Đây là ảnh minh hoạ dùng chung cho dữ liệu sân mẫu, không phải ảnh chụp các sân thật.
- Ảnh được đóng gói trong ứng dụng nên không cần mạng khi hiển thị thẻ sân.

Prompt cuối cùng:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: local landscape photograph for the top of a mobile football-field management card. Create one natural, believable documentary photograph of a small Vietnamese 5-a-side artificial-turf football pitch at blue hour, shot from an elevated spectator balcony at a slightly diagonal overhead angle. Show the full rectangular green pitch with white touchlines, center circle, small goals, dark perimeter net fencing and bright floodlights, a few distant amateur players, and modest neighboring buildings. Realistic proportions, fine turf texture, candid sports-facility photography, no advertising polish. Landscape composition, wide framing suited to a 2.4:1 card crop, pitch centered, with clear corners and no distracting foreground. No text, no typography, no labels, no UI, no logos, no watermark, no collages. This is a standalone photograph, not an image of a phone screen.
